Why this version plays better

An opening shot that sells the world

The camera sweeps over the valley before you take control, so you see the windmill, the giant tree and the waterfall and want to go there.

PLAYER_MOTIVATION §4

Landmarks you navigate by

Every region has one tall silhouette against the sky. You set your own goals by looking, not by reading markers.

PLAYER_MOTIVATION §3

Something every 30–45 seconds

Stars, creatures, views, planks and named places are spread so that a walk in any direction finds something.

PLAYER_MOTIVATION §2

Quests that pull you through the world

Rowan's planks are hidden near three landmarks, so the first quest becomes a tour of the valley.

ENGAGEMENT_DESIGN §4

Gates you can see past

The river blocks the east until the bridge is fixed, and deep water stays closed until you learn to swim in Level 05.

ENGAGEMENT_DESIGN §2

Realistic light, stylized shapes

Golden-hour sun, sky-coloured ambient light, soft shadows, haze and wind-blown grass. Friendly proportions stay.

PLAYER_MOTIVATION §7 (ADR-012, proposed)

Reference images I'm asking for

For each card, collect 1–3 images that match the brief and save them in Art/Concept/. The thumbnails are colour guides from the art palette, not the images themselves.

Where to find them: your own sketches, commissioned concept art, or paid asset-store packs whose licence you have read. Private mood boards are fine for inspiration only. Nothing from another company's game goes into the build.
